# 3D films — a product model, thousands of instances, a chain as f(t)

One film so far: **domino** (`cases/domino-25s`, accepted as v8, 2026-09-27). 12,346 phones fall
in 25.6 s; one phone becomes one pixel, and the same picture resolves 1 → 3×3 → 10×10 → 32×32 → 100×100 until the one
phone left standing shows it whole. Everything below is what that film measured or had rejected. The contract is the
2D one (`__seek`, `__ready`, `__meta`, `__track`, `__motion`, `__events`); only the drawing is three.js.

> The domino film's source (the comp, the Blender scripts, the score) is not part of the public release — the public
> `cases/domino-25s/` ships the film and its breakdown. File names below describe how it was made.

## The pipeline

| stage | tool | output |
|---|---|---|
| model | Blender through the MCP bridge (`blender/bmcp.py` → addon socket :9876) — model only, never animation or lighting | `P18_hero` / `P18_mid` / `P18_lo` in one scene, one material slot per part |
| export | `blender/export_glb.py` (Z up, one primitive per slot) | `assets/p18.glb` → base64 → `assets/p18.glb.js` (`window.P18_GLB`) |
| light | a generated softbox equirect | `assets/studio_env.hdr` → `assets/studio_env.hdr.js` (`window.STUDIO_HDR`) |
| three | r180 + addons bundled as a classic IIFE (`vendor/three-entry.js`) | `vendor/three.bundle.js` → `window.THREE`, `window.THREEX` |
| comp | one HTML file, `lib/motion.js` for curves | `comp.html` |
| render | `render.py --gpu` | 1080p30 drafts, `--final` 4K60 |

**Why the bundle.** Chrome blocks ES modules and `fetch` under `file://`, which is how render.py opens a comp. So three
and the addons the comp uses (GLTFLoader, HDRLoader, EffectComposer, RenderPass, UnrealBloomPass, BokehPass, OutputPass …)
are bundled once:

```bash
esbuild vendor/three-entry.js --bundle --format=iife --minify --outfile=vendor/three.bundle.js   # from a dir with node_modules/three
```

and every binary asset is inlined as base64 in a `.js` file (`HDRLoader().load('data:…;base64,' + STUDIO_HDR)`,
`GLTFLoader().parse(Uint8Array.from(atob(P18_GLB), …).buffer)`).

**`--gpu` is not optional.** Headless Chromium draws WebGL on SwiftShader: 652 ms a capture for 11k instanced PBR boxes
with bloom. `--use-angle=metal --enable-gpu --ignore-gpu-blocklist` (`render.py --gpu`) puts it on
the Apple GPU: 168 ms, about the screenshot's own cost. Measured on the film: 10,000 × the 1.3k-tri
LOD = 12.6 M triangles in 9 instanced draws, with bloom, 185 ms a capture; a 768-frame 1080p draft is 4,300–4,500
captures, 98–119 s on 6 workers. 4K60 is ~2× the frames at 4× the pixels: estimate 12–15 minutes and say so first.

## The scene

- **World in metres, Z up** (Blender's axes, so the GLB's too). Everything hangs off `ROOT` (rotation.x = −π/2) and
  `Z2Y(x, y, z) = (x, z, −y)` converts for the camera. An equirect env is upright only this way.
- **The set the user wanted**: 「白色…极简优雅的白色浅色纯色的3d空间」. A seamless white cyc: the floor is an unlit plane in
  the background colour exactly, a `ShadowMaterial` plane on top carries the key's soft shadow, fog in the same colour
  from ~2× to ~9× the camera distance. The dark glossy reflective floor planned first was dropped before it was built.
- **One key that follows the look point**, its shadow camera sized by the camera's distance (`ext`), a 4096 map; a soft
  hemisphere fill; the HDR for reflections only. `NeutralToneMapping`, bloom 0.4, Bokeh only in the macro shots.
- **Variety by instance, not by model**: one neutral GLB; the four finishes (Black, Silver, Glacier, Burgundy) are a
  per-instance colour on the aluminium / back glass / lens ring parts, seeded — the user's worry was 「不同的颜色…不然
  太单调」.

## Thousands of instances

Per level of detail, one `InstancedMesh` per material part, and **the parts of a level share one matrix buffer, one
finish buffer and one screen buffer** (`im.instanceMatrix = L.mat`), so a phone is written once per seek. Every seek:

1. `angles(t)` for every phone (below);
2. project each phone's position; drop what is off frame (a margin that grows as it nears the camera);
3. by distance to the eye pick the level (that film: the nearest ≤ 24 within 0.9 m → hero 56k tris, ≤ 4,000 within 3.2 m
   → mid 5k, the rest lo 1.3k — sized to its model and camera, measure yours);
4. `fill(level, ids, t)`: the matrix from heading ψ and tilt θ written by hand, the finish, the screen; `count = ids.length`.

No ≤ 300-tri LOD was needed. `frustumCulled = false` on the instanced meshes (three's culling uses the base geometry).

**The screen is a shader, not a texture.** `MeshBasicMaterial` black (the user: no reflections on screens, ever) with
`onBeforeCompile`: per-instance `aScr = (r, g, b, glow)`, masked in local millimetres by the measured active area —
1.75 mm in from the silhouette with the body's own n = 3 superellipse corner, minus the 16 × 5.8 mm island 7.4 mm from
the top. glow < −0.5 switches to a canvas texture (the survivor shows the whole picture). The bezel was the user's
hardest note on the model (「这些屏幕边框怎么这么大 这是最难受的」): a plain rounded rect 2.35 mm in, not concentric
with the body, reads as a thick frame.

## The chain as f(t) — no physics

A rigid-body sim of 12,000 phones is slow, not repeatable and can't be told "land on beat 20". Instead the film lays out
**when each phone is struck**, then computes every angle from geometry.

- `phone(x, y, heading, tStrike, opts)` records one; `link(i, j)` makes j the one i falls onto (spacing s from positions).
- `line(path, L, t0, v, {tEnd, acc})` puts phones every GAP of arc length along a path, struck at `t0 + g(u)·L/v`.
  `tEnd` paces the front to arrive on a beat (v = L / (tEnd − t0)); `acc` makes it start slow and surge into its end,
  `g(u) = u + acc·u(1−u)` — at 0.6 a front triples its speed into the beat, which is what made the tree's splits read.
- `block(cols, rows, x0, y0, tOf)`: columns of lines; `tOf(c, r) = t0 + hypot(dx, dy) / v` gives a circular front from
  the struck point. A branch split is two lines from one tip, the second linked as `s2`.
- Every section boundary is a written time: `SEC.hit0 = beat(6)`, `SEC.s0 = beat(8)`, `SEC.b10 = beat(16)`, splits on
  beats 20–28, `SEC.f0 = beat(39)`; the spans between are solved for speeds (`v3 = 2·GAP / (beat(8) − 0.06 − hit0)`).
  A retime is a constant.
- `angles(t)` walks the phones **in reverse** (every successor has a larger index): standing before its strike; struck,
  a free swing to the contact angle θc = asin((s − D)/H) on the profile a·u + (1−a)·u^2.2; once the next one is struck,
  the lean rule on the next one's angle, **θk = θn + asin((s·cos θn − D)/H)**; the last of a line swings to π/2 − 0.03
  and settles with a small damped bounce. D and H are the phone's thickness and height.
- Flags on a phone: `stand` (the survivor; removed ones leave a gap), `lit` (how its screen lights: a line's comet, a
  block's picture on landing, dark until the drop), `grp` (block or line, for sound).
- Debug: a trigger-order check (`T0[next] > T0[i]`) at build; `?t=…&hud` prints the LOD counts.

## The camera

Per section a function of t returns `{eye, look, fov, roll}`; between sections, ease from one set-up to the next with
the library's curves, key the look a little ahead of the subject so the camera follows rather than leads, add
`OM.shake` on the hits, and in `seek` apply roll by rotating `CAM.up` about the view axis.

- **Follow the front, not the block**: `frontOn(ids, t)` interpolates the struck position along a list of phones;
  `MAIN` is the front the viewer follows from the first phone to the field, `HERO` the branch the camera rides.
- **Make each section's set-up a function** (`set32(t)`) so the section before can land on it exactly.
- **Top-down lost the chain.** 「航拍俯拍，就看不出来多米诺骨牌那种连贯性了，俯拍啥也看不出来」: from above, a falling chain
  is a colour change on a map; from low it is phones pushing phones. That film then stayed under ~35°. Ask what has to be
  read in each shot and pick the height from that.
- **On the beat**: what failed and what worked for the tree is in rhythm.md; it is one film's answer.
- **Text in the world must be read, not just seen.** Words printed flat on a floor (or any plane at an angle) shrink on
  screen to height × sin(elevation) and slant away along their baseline; at a low ¾ camera a 9 mm pixel-mono keyword came
  out ~15 px and half-squashed — 「看不清楚是什么关键词」. What fixed the domino insert (one film's answer, not a set-up to
  copy): the keyword itself made big and the call name small/muted, emphasis on the keyword only; while each line typed,
  the camera rode that line from higher up, square to its baseline, framed so the phone it built stayed in shot; words and
  object took turns rather than competing. The general move is: know which word is the point of this beat, and let the
  camera arrive somewhere that reads it during the beat — by a rise, a ride, a pass, a landing, whatever fits the film —
  then carry on. Measure it: expose `window.__legible(t)` → for each keyword on during its read window, the projected em
  height in px and squash (screen height / screen width of an em square); that film held ≥ 24 px and ≥ 0.6 and measured
  40–80 px / 0.7–0.8 after the fix. A legibility pass can cost the rest ratio (camera moving on every line) — say so;
  don't buy stillness with an unreadable word.
- `__track` = the followed front's and the survivor's screen boxes from their 8 corners at their current angle;
  `__meta.inFrame` names them; `__motion` is a ring of points around the look plus the look's travel.

## Sound from the comp

`__events()` returns the named hits (tip, first landing, the 3×3 notes, splits, drop, field, blinks, dive, off) with a
pan from the projected x, **every contact** binned at 100 Hz (count, mean pan, how many belong to blocks), and each
line contact as a `clack`. `dump_events.py` → `events.json` → `score.py`: accents from the clacks, a grain swarm from
the bins, the music on the comp's grid. Why the swarm is shaped the way it is: sound.md, *Dense chains — the frog*.

## The model through the Blender MCP

Three rounds were rejected before 「可以了 我觉得模型问题搞定了」. Root causes, in order:

1. **A card, not a phone**: front and back as single n-gons with a bevel modifier, smooth-shaded — shading gradients and
   triangle fans in glTF; the camera plateau a box stuck on the back; the look living in node shaders that don't export.
   The rebuild: one cross-section profile (glass edge, gasket, lip, band, back roll-over) swept along superellipse
   corners with analytic normals; side bands with real holes (buttons in recesses, Camera Control, USB-C, speaker
   holes); the plateau cut into the back with a fillet and roll-over; lenses as revolved stacks (ring with a chamfer,
   collar, cover glass, terraces, a domed element); antenna gaps as rings in the sweep.
2. **Measure, don't recall**: Apple's product-viewer stills (the near-orthographic front + back at 7.1 px/mm) gave the
   lens centres, ring diameters, plateau rim, antenna gaps and island to 0.1 mm; the four finishes' colours from the
   colour stills. The first colours were the previous model's.
3. **Proportions of the back glass**: inset 5 mm it read as a card in a frame; the real one runs to ~3.7 mm of the edge.
4. **Staging in the model review**: lamps and a black board washed it out — 「先不要在里面做加光的还有一块黑木板这些，
   就悬空」. Review stills float on black, lit only by the env.

Blender gotchas: open it with `open -a Blender` outside the sandbox (a sandboxed launch never opens the port); run
`reset.py` after a restart (the default cube swallows the model); don't switch the viewport out of Cycles while a
script renders (deadlock in `ccl::Session::cancel`); `read_factory_settings` kills the addon server; export with every
slot on DATA and restore any visibility and override the export touched.
